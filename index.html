Sawasdee

Welcome. Ban Lom Jen is our family homestay in Ban Rimkok, Mae Yao, about 15 minutes from Chiang Rai city and about 25 minutes from the airport. The house sits in rice and corn fields beside the Mae Kok, with the mountains in front of it.

We started building at the end of 2011 and finished just after Songkran 2012. If you are staying, you can cook with the family.

Two buildings: the family house and the guest rooms. Four superior rooms. Free Wi-Fi and laundry. Motorbikes and bicycles if you tell us a little ahead. Parking is free, there is a locked room for your own bicycle, and we can drive you to the airport or the bus station.

Cooking with Son

In the morning she suggests the menu. Then you walk the vegetable and herb garden and see what is ready.

What the garden does not have, you buy at a local market. That walk is part of the class.

You cook in the outdoor kitchen, or indoors if the weather turns. Yam woon sen, pad krapao, nam prik ong, kao pad nehm, and curries. You take a pack of spices and pastes home.

Trips

Every route below starts and ends here.

Motorbike

A Honda Click 115cc automatic, for two adults. We can set a route to how you ride.

  • Karen Elephant Village, Hanging Bridge, Ban Pha Soet hot springs, a waterfall, Doi Incee. About 50 km of asphalt and a short dirt stretch, with some hills. The elephant village, the Mae Kok, the hot-springs restaurant, the waterfall, and the Big Buddha.
  • Doi Incee, the Big Buddha, Ban Boon Rawd (Singha farm: tea, fruit, a restaurant), Nam Tok Khun Kon, Wat Rong Khun. About 60 km, climbs at the start. The Buddha is 700 steps up. Khun Kon is a 1.5 km hike, and the ride is still worth it if you skip the walk.
  • Wat Rong Khun, a Chiang Rai city tour, Chiang Rai Beach. About 50 km, little climbing. The Golden Clock, King Mengrai, temples, the Tessaban Market, and the beach.

Personalized trip

Tell us how far you want to go and what you want to see. We'll shape the day around that.

Bicycle

ATB mountain bikes with front suspension and 24 gears. You get a map, and a guide if you want one. Most rides suit an unhurried day. One does not.

  • Karen Elephant Village, Hanging Bridge, Ban Pha Soet hot springs. About 40 km. Medium to heavy.
  • Doi Incee, the Big Buddha, Ban Boon Rawd, Wat Rong Khun. About 45 km. Medium.
  • Chiang Rai city and Chiang Rai Beach. About 35 km, little climbing. Easy to medium. The Golden Clock, King Mengrai, temples, the Tessaban Market.
  • Mae Yao to Huai Mae Sai. About 25 km and a lot of uphill. Heavy. Add the elephant village for about 15 km, or a short city loop for about 20 km.

Personalized trip

Trekking

Pickup by car or songthaew. Bring mosquito spray and a swimming suit. Drinks are not included.

  • One day. Pickup 8:30. About 30 minutes to an Akha village, then a river, tea, banana, and coffee gardens, bamboo, and highland rice. Lunch is sticky rice cooked in bamboo, pork barbecue, and eggplant salad, and you can help cook it. Then a waterfall, and about 1.5 hours back to the village and the car.
  • Two days, one night. Pickup 9:00, via Baan Huay Khom. Rice and pineapple fields, bamboo, a waterfall, then an Akha village for the night. Dinner with the family, then hill-tribe stories and games. Next morning at 8:30, more gardens, bamboo cooking for lunch, and a hot spring before the city.
  • Three days, two nights. Overnight in an Akha village, then Huay Mae Sai waterfall and up Doi Bor (about 1,000 metres) with a bamboo lunch on the way. The second night is a camp with Lahu food and a view over Chiang Rai. The last morning walks down to the car, then a hot spring, back around 15:30.

Car

A private car with air-conditioning, and a driver who also guides and speaks English. Each day below runs from 9:00 to 17:00 and comes back here. The days end the same way up north: Mae Sai at the Thai-Myanmar border, the Golden Triangle where Myanmar, Laos, and Thailand meet on the Mekong, the opium museum or a short market crossing into Laos, and the Chiang Saen ruins and temples (1260).

  • White Temple, Black House, Long Neck Karen, Akha and Lahu villages, then Mae Sai, the Golden Triangle, and Chiang Saen.
  • Doi Mae Salong and the Kuomintang Yunnan tea settlement, with a chance to stop in Yao and Akha villages on the way to Mae Sai.
  • Doi Tung: the Royal Villa of the late Princess Mother, Mae Fah Luang, her gardens, and the house that is now a museum. Then the same northern finish.

2 people: 2,500 baht

That price is for the day in the car, not for a night in a room.
